## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Contextual in-product help and plain language

The portal SHALL provide contextual, in-product help that answers common questions before they are asked and SHALL present copy in plain language rather than jargon, treating support volume as a design problem.

#### Scenario: Contextual FAQ answers before a ticket

- **GIVEN** a guardian on the financial section who is unsure how to pay a boleto
- **WHEN** they view the section
- **THEN** a contextual in-product FAQ/help affordance explains the action inline, without requiring a support ticket

#### Scenario: Plain-language copy over jargon

- **GIVEN** a portal screen that previously used technical or legalese terms
- **WHEN** a guardian reads it
- **THEN** the copy is in plain language understandable by a non-technical user
